Ask Claude to scout

Claude searches the web for places that aren't on the map yet, checks they're within 5 miles of Peckham, and adds them with a review.

Ask Claude to review

Claude reads recent public write-ups (council pages, local news, parent blogs), then writes a short review with an Adventure Score out of 10, highlights, watch-outs and links.

See requests in progress on GitHub. Only requests from the site owner's GitHub account are acted on.

Adventure is everywhere. Park playgrounds come from Southwark Council's play-area list. "Our picks" are places we've been ourselves. AI reviews are written by Claude from recent public web sources, not visits, and link to those sources. Walking times are estimates: straight-line distance plus 30%, at 80 m a minute.
